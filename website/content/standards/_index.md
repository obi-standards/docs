# Standards

OBIS standards are published as numbered documents under the identifier scheme `OBIS-NNNN`. Each carries a state: **Draft**, **Public Review**, or **Published**. The lifecycle is defined in [OBIS-0001]({{< relref "OBIS-0001" >}}).

The catalog is organised around the three initial focus areas (investigations and forensics, regulatory reporting, and open analytics) plus a small body of process documents. It will grow as drafts are entered into the lifecycle.
